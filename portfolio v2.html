Hey, I'm Ashish. I design enterprise software that people use all day.

For over 12 years, I’ve been designing at the intersection of complex data systems, enterprise B2B platforms, and human behavior. Currently, I serve as CUX Lead at Evalueserve, where I manage a global team of senior designers across four countries and architect the design systems and AI workflows that keep our products running fast, modern, and aligned.

I sit with the people doing the job, map what they actually do, then design the smallest interface that removes the manual part. When AI is in the picture, the real design problem is usually trust: showing the source, the confidence, and the way back to a human decision.

How I work

01
I sit with the work first
Shadowing sessions, workflow maps, and the real people have quietly built to survive the current tool.
02
Then I frame the decision
What is being judged, on what evidence, by whom. Enterprise UX is mostly decision design wearing a table.
03
I prototype at real density
Ten rows of dummy data proves nothing. I test with the volume and messiness of the real thing.
04
Ship, then watch the usage
Adoption data decides the next iteration, not the loudest opinion in the review.
