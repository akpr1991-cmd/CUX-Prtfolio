About & process

I lead conversational and enterprise UX at Evalueserve, working with analysts, credit officers and procurement teams. Most of my work starts inside someone else's spreadsheet: I sit with the people doing the job, map what they actually do, then design the smallest interface that removes the manual part.

When AI is involved, the design problem is usually trust — showing the source, the confidence and the way back to a human decision.

01
Sit with the work
Shadowing, workflow maps, the real spreadsheets.
02
Frame the decision
What is being judged, with what evidence, by whom.
03
Prototype at real density
Enterprise screens are tested with enterprise data.
04
Ship, then measure usage
Adoption data decides the next iteration.
